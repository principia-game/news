On the 6th of August 2022, the source code of Principia was pushed onto GitHub as open source, allowing the community to continue development of the game where it had left off in late 2014. Today marks four years since that date, and in that time the game has seen a lot of changes and improvements, growing beyond its previous fate as proprietary abandonware.

To celebrate this occasion, let's take a look back at the game's journey as an open source project so far.

## The early days
The initial work centered around getting the game working again across platforms, removing license verification and other functionality that is no longer relevant for an open source project, as well as fixing up the previously unreleased Linux version of the game.

In September of 2022, the new [Principia Wiki](https://principia-web.se/wiki/) was launched on this website, providing a new home for information about the game. Content from the old wiki would gradually be imported into it, and knowledge maintained by the community up until that point about the game's objects were all merged together into a single canonical source going forward.

In addition to this, an archive of all the levels that were available on the old Principia community site ran by Bithack was made available, bringing back tens of thousands of levels made by the community between 2013-2018 that were previously thought to be lost.

On the very last day of 2022, [2022.12.31 was released](/news/8) as the first officially open source release of Principia, being labelled as a beta build of 1.5.2 at the time. Unused objects that were previously inaccessible and traded around in the community as secrets (Magnet, Gear, Angular Damper...) were made available right in the sandbox menu, among many other things.

## 2023
[Principia 2023.12.26](/wiki/Changelog#2023-12-26-1-5-2-beta-2023-12-26) brought new LuaScript functions and raised some limits in the game. On desktop the game's dialogs were upgraded from GTK2 to GTK3, much to the delight of Linux distribution maintainers.

## 2024
[Principia 2024.02.29](/wiki/Changelog#2024-02-29) marked the first release with the current calendar versioning system. Official Linux AppImage builds were introduced and the Android version of the game was finally made available on F-Droid. An experimental port of the game to Haiku OS was merged into the source tree, beginning the trend of expanding the game to new and interesting platforms.

<figure>
	<img src="/news/images/32/principia_haiku_first.webp" style="width:860px" alt="The first screenshot of Principia running on Haiku OS back in 2023, showing the procedural adventure terrain in the sandbox with a robot configuration dialog open. [Principia 2024.06.28](/wiki/Changelog#2024-06-28) saw further improvements across the game and across platforms. The version of SDL2 was updated to the latest on Android, and more new LuaScript functions were added. In addition to this, an experimental port of the game to macOS was made available.

	<img src="/news/images/32/principia_macos.webp" style="width:960px" alt="Principia on macOS playing the Art Adventure level (ID 1294 on the archive). There is a terminal on the side showing the Principia executable being called to play the level, and the macOS dock is visible on the bottom.">

Shortly thereafter [Principia 2024.07.12](/news/16) was released to fix some issues with the previous release, as well as a fix allowing the game to run on postmarketOS, a Linux distribution for mobile devices based on Alpine Linux.

## 2025
The next year, [Principia 2025.04.05](/news/19) was released bringing back the long forgotten Puzzle level type as a proper one that can be created in the sandbox again. In addition to this the play menu was updated to better reflect the current state of the game, and a large amount of bugs were fixed.

In June of 2025, the [Principia YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@principiagame/videos) began uploading videos showcasing various community levels for most of the summer, bringing new attention to the game and its rich community content.

Later in autumn, the archive of levels from the old community site that were previously available under a separate subdomain archive.principia-web.se was merged into the main community site, integrating it better into the main site and making it easier to find interesting levels.

At the end of 2025, the previously non-functional port of Principia to the web was fixed up to the point where it could now play levels on the community site. A new link subsequently appeared on every level page on the community site, allowing you to play levels right inside your web browser without needing to install anything.

## 2026
The Principia community site saw more improvements in the beginning of 2026. The popular/top/latest levels pages were merged into one along with the archive pages, creating a new levels page with all these options right at your fingertips and continuing the work of making the levels in the archive more accessible.

<figure>
	<img src="/news/images/32/browse_levels.webp" style="width:640px" alt="Screenshot of the new level browse page, containing radio buttons to select whether you should see current levels or levels in the archive, as well as a dropdown to sort by (latest, oldest, most liked and most downloaded) and filter by level type (all, custom, adventure and puzzle).">
</figure>

In early June, [Principia 2026.06.06](/news/28) was released with all changes that had accumulated since the last release. This included a new user data structure and location across platforms, and the elusive Gearbox object from the game's early development was finally fixed up to the point where it could be placed into a level without crashing the game.

<figure>
	<img src="/news/images/28/gearbox.webp" style="width:500px" alt="Screenshot of the gearbox placed down into a level. There are two stickies surrounding it with the texts 'ZOMG!!' and 'GEARBOX???'">
</figure>

Then Principia 2026.06.19 was released as a minor hotfix release. Independent of the release, Principia was also made to run on Windows XP again, a process which started with building a custom toolchain for Windows XP and then producing custom Principia builds made to run on XP. This accomplishment made its way onto the front page of [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48718995) and got a lot of attention across the internet.

In July, [Principia 2026.07.09](/news/30) was released with the game being upgraded to use SDL3 across all platforms, and the first dialogs implemented using Dear Imgui rather than GTK appears.

<figure>
	<img src="/news/images/30/sandbox_menu_imgui.webp" alt="Screenshot of the new sandbox menu popup dialog using Dear Imgui.">
</figure>

In addition to this, the game's touch support was made fully configurable behind a runtime setting, greatly improving the usability of the game on touch devices outside of Android. Along with a runtime setting `use_gles` to switch to OpenGL ES, this makes the game more versatile for running on a variety of devices, such as devices running postmarketOS or other non-Android mobile Linux distributions.

Shortly thereafter, [Principia 2026.07.15](/news/31) was released as a minor hotfix release, fixing some critical issues with the previous release.

But... about that migration from GTK to Dear Imgui... [are we Imgui yet?](https://principia-web.se/imgui) Yes! On the 20th of July, the final GTK dialog in the game was replaced with a new one implemented using Dear Imgui, finishing the plan to free the game from GTK entirely. This will soon be appearing in a future release of Principia coming later this month.

The migration from GTK to Dear Imgui meant many usability and portability improvements for the game, such as the sandbox being fully usable in the web version of Principia. Now you can finally build and [publish levels](https://principia-web.se/level/1663) straight from your web browser without needing to install anything.

<figure>
	<img src="/news/images/31/upload_dialog_web.webp" style="width:540px" alt="Principia running in the web browser, my username is visible in the top-left corner and the Imgui level publish dialog is visible.">
</figure>

Just recently, the game was also made to run on the Nintendo Switch with devkitPro's homebrew toolchain. While this port is still in its early stages, the Dear Imgui-based dialogs are critical to being able to port the game to more embedded platforms where GTK is not available, and the Switch is one such platform.

<figure>

	<figcaption><a href="https://principia-web.se/level/1683">See the level that was published from a Switch emulator with your own eyes!</a></figcaption>

Also coming in the pipeline are improvements in the game's rendering pipeline, allowing for gamma correction to make the game beautiful on all platforms and bloom to be available everywhere as a setting. This will later pave the way for potential future new graphical effects added to the game when a cleaner postprocessing pipeline is in place.

## Today
All this brings us to the present day. Principia has seen huge improvements in these four years, and all this work has been driven by a single individual, from the wiki to the community site and the game itself. Me, ROllerozxa.

I originally began hosting principia-web in late 2020, originally as a revival site for the game but it has since become the open source Principia's main community site. I was integral to the early days of the open source project, and have served as the project maintainer ever since. I work on this in my free time with no financial compensation outside of donations. Sometimes I wish I could give more to the project, but I am still happy with the direction at which the game is heading.

I've accepted donations for some years now and have reminded people to donate to support the project. But for this day, I would like to try something different and set a donation goal for this month.
