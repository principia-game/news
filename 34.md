Back in 2024, I released a printable cheatsheet reference of the Principia LuaScript API [onto itch.io as a paid purchase](https://rollerozxa.itch.io/principia-luascript-api-cheatsheet). I originally charged $2.99 for it, but later lowered the price to $1.99.

The idea was that sales for the cheatsheet would support the game. Unfortunately the interest in it wasn't as large as I had hoped, but I would still thank the single person who bought the cheatsheet at the discounted $1.99 price last year.

Now, thanks to 5 extremely amazing people who supported Principia during the [August 2026 Donation Drive](/august-2026-donation-drive) and reached our goal of 1000 SEK, I am happy to announce that the LuaScript cheatsheet will be made free for everyone to download. In fact, just like Principia it will be free _and_ open source, licensed under the CC-BY 4.0 license.

But unlike Principia, the actual open source release of the cheatsheet will not take years until it actually happens... In fact, it is already here! Download both versions of the cheatsheet (colour and greyscale) at the new page for the cheatsheet below:

## [principia-web.se/luascript-api-cheatsheet](https://principia-web.se/luascript-api-cheatsheet)

The cheatsheet was made with HTML+CSS and rendered into a PDF with Weasyprint, and all the source files for the cheatsheet are [available in this GitHub repository](https://github.com/principia-game/luascript-cheatsheet). If you want to make your own cheatsheet for something else with a similar style, then you are free to do so under the CC-BY 4.0 license.
